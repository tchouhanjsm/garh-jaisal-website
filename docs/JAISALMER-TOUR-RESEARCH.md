# Jaisalmer tour research — candidate itinerary themes

**Purpose:** identify differentiated tour concepts grounded in official destination information, then test which the hotel can actually operate. This is not a public product list or confirmation that Garh Jaisal Haveli can arrange every route.

| Candidate theme | Destination evidence | Why evaluate it | Must confirm before selling |
|---|---|---|---|
| Havelis and sandstone architecture walk | Rajasthan Tourism lists Patwon Ki Haveli, Nathmal Ji Ki Haveli and Salim Singh Ki Haveli. | Low-complexity extension of an existing guided city tour. | Stops that can be entered, guide, route, duration, entry fees and price. |
| Jain temples and Fort architecture | Official tourism places the Jain temples inside Jaisalmer Fort and describes their stone-carving heritage. | Distinct architecture/culture theme. | Visiting hours, etiquette, photography rules, guide permissions, entry/access requirements and price. |
| Gadisar Lake / Bada Bagh / Vyas Chhatri photography outing | Official Jaisalmer sources describe Gadisar Lake and Bada Bagh/Vyas Chhatri viewpoints. | Photo-focused itinerary for landscape and sandstone architecture. These stops may need separate outings. | Feasible route, access/timings, sunset seasonality, transport, fees and total time. |
| Kuldhara heritage and desert landscape | Rajasthan Tourism identifies Kuldhara as an abandoned heritage village/protected monument. | Adds heritage-and-landscape interest alongside existing desert safari themes, without sensational “haunted” claims. | Visitor access/closing rules, transport, stops, guide, fees, route and price. |
| Natural history: Akal Wood Fossil Park or Desert National Park area | Official tourism describes Akal Wood Fossil Park and the Thar ecosystem/wildlife at Desert National Park. | Differentiated geology/nature theme. Treat these as separate options unless an operator verifies a combined route. | Access, permissions/guide requirements, transport, route, heat/weather plan, fees and price. Never guarantee wildlife sightings. |
| Longewala War Memorial history excursion | Rajasthan Tourism has a dedicated memorial page and historical background on the 1971 battle. | History-focused option distinct from city walks/desert safaris; operationally the most demanding candidate. | Whether the hotel can arrange it; current operating days/hours, visitor requirements, road/route conditions, total travel time, transport, permissions/fees and price. Do not imply access to restricted border areas. |

## Suggested priority

1. **Havelis & architecture** — likely easiest to validate as an extension of the existing city-guide concept.
2. **Jain temples & Fort architecture** — requires confirmation of temple visitor rules and timing.
3. **Gadisar / Bada Bagh photo route** — verify itinerary before promising a sunset slot.
4. **Kuldhara heritage & landscape** — check operating rules and transport.
5. **Akal Fossil Park / Desert National Park** — a differentiated nature option, conditional on access and responsible viewing.
6. **Longewala War Memorial** — enquiry-only until travel logistics and visitor requirements are verified.

## Website guardrails

- Display these as **ideas to enquire about**, not bookable packages.
- Each link opens a prefilled Contact enquiry and does not reserve a tour.
- Do not publish rates, durations, start times, distances, inclusions, transport, guide languages, reviews or permissions until the owner confirms them.
- Existing public routes and live tours remain unchanged. Longewala/Rajasthan detail pages remain draft until owner facts are answered.
