# Content Studio: staff guide (one page)

1. Double-click **Start-Studio** (`Start-Studio.bat` on Windows, `Start-Studio.command` on Mac). Your browser opens. Keep the black window open; close it when done.
2. **Title**: type the name of the tour or guide. Press Next.
3. **Photos**: drag photos in, or click to choose them. Press Build and check.
4. **Check**: green is good. Amber or red lines say in plain words what to fix. Go Back to change things.
5. For a tour, tick the box only if you confirmed price, timings and inclusions with the hotel today.
6. Type your name, press **Looks good, save it**, then **Send for review**.

Nothing goes live until the owner publishes it. Nothing is ever deleted. Ignore "Owner tools": those are for the owner.
